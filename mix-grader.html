Mix Grader
Browser-native DJ mix analysis & quality scoring — v1.4.0
What it does

Mix Grader analyzes a recorded DJ mix and scores it across four technical dimensions — transitions, energy arc, gain staging, and BPM consistency — producing an overall letter grade and a detailed breakdown of every detectable event in the recording.

It runs entirely in the browser using the Web Audio API. No files are uploaded anywhere. Your mix stays on your machine from start to finish.

Analysis modules
BPM Detection
Autocorrelation on the onset envelope across the full mix, plus per-segment analysis on both sides of each detected transition. Octave correction prevents 88 BPM reading as 176.
Transition Detection
Finds local energy minima below an adaptive threshold. Classifies each as Blend, Quick mix, or Hard cut based on dip depth and width. Grades using blend quality, BPM drift, and transition type.
Energy Arc
RMS measured in 0.5-second windows across the full duration. Scored on coefficient of variation — rewards dynamic mixes that build and breathe, penalizes flat or chaotic level profiles.
Gain & Clipping
Walks every sample checking for 0dBFS events. Reports clip count, clip ratio, and an approximate LUFS loudness value. Scored against an ideal broadcast-safe range of −14 to −8 LUFS.
Dead Air Detection
Scans for consecutive windows below a genre-adjusted silence threshold. Events over 1.5 seconds are flagged and classified Minor, Major, or Critical by duration.
Waveform & Zoom
Full PCM waveform drawn behind the energy arc. Click or drag on the timeline to open a high-resolution zoom view of any region — useful for inspecting individual transitions up close.
Honest caveats
Technical execution only. Mix Grader grades what's in the signal — beatmatching, gain staging, dead air, energy flow. It cannot grade taste, track selection, crowd reading, or whether the programming was emotionally right. Most bad mixes fail on the technical side first anyway, so the signal is still meaningful.
No track identification. Recognizing individual tracks requires fingerprinting APIs (ACRCloud, AudD) which would require sending audio to an external server. That's outside scope by design.
Streaming URLs not supported. Mixcloud, SoundCloud, and similar platforms use DRM or token-protected streams the browser cannot access. Drop a local file (MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, M4A, OGG).
Large WAV files. decodeAudioData() expands compressed audio to raw PCM in memory. A 200MB MP3 becomes ~700MB of Float32 samples. Works fine on modern hardware — may be slow or memory-constrained on older devices.
Scoring & weights

The four module scores are combined into a weighted overall grade. Default weights are Transitions 35% · Energy Arc 25% · Gain/Clip 20% · BPM Consistency 20%, reflecting that transitions are the most immediately audible quality signal in a DJ mix.

Weights are fully configurable via the sliders above the grade card. Five genre presets (Open Format, R&B/Hip-Hop, House/Techno, Drum & Bass, Ambient/Downtempo) apply tuned defaults including adjusted BPM ranges and silence thresholds.
